  • Disrespectful behavior will not be tolerated; your commission will be cancelled immediately if this occurs.
  • The terms outlined here may vary slightly depending on the specific type of commission discussed.

II. DO'S & DONT'TS

I WILL Draw:

I WILL NOT Draw:

III. PAYMENT & FEES

  • Payment is split into two parts: a 50% non-refundable down-payment to start the commission, and the remaining 50% due before the painting/coloring stage begins.
  • All payments are processed via PayPal Invoice (USD).
  • A rush fee may be charged for tight deadlines, provided my schedule allows for it.
  • Exact prices for commissions and fees depend on the complexity and type of the request.

IV. CLIENT OBLIGATIONS

  • Please write in a clear and concise manner. Professionalism is appreciated.
  • Provide multiple, clear reference images of the character or subject.
  • Mood boards are welcome and encouraged!
  • You assume responsibility for providing all necessary information. I am not responsible for dissatisfaction caused by missing details or unclear descriptions/references provided by the client.
  • It is your responsibility to carefully read through the T.O.S. and Commission Information.

V. PROCESS & WORK SCHEDULE

  • Artwork Resolutions depends on the commission type.
    • Character-oriented: A4 Portrait (2480x3508 px).
    • Background-oriented: A4 Landscape (3508x2480 px).
  • I will send a sketch for approval before proceeding.
  • Work-in-Progress (WIP) updates will be shared via your preferred platform (Twitter, Instagram, or Discord).
  • Sketches and in-progress shots will be sent as low-resolution, watermarked images.
  • Specific deadlines must be discussed and agreed upon prior to payment.
  • I will do my best to complete commissions in a timely manner. However, personal circumstances may occasionally cause delays. I will notify you immediately if any issues arise.

VI. REVISIONS

  • Revisions are unlimited and free in the sketching stage.
  • Only 2 revisions are allowed after the sketching stage. Any additional revisions at this stage will incur an extra fee, regardless of complexity.
  • Revisions required due to an error on my part are always free, regardless of the stage.
  • I reserve the right to determine what constitutes a "minor" or "major" revision.

  • All characters portrayed remain the copyright of their respective owners.
  • The final product is for Personal Use Only unless commercial rights are purchased.
    • Commercial rights incur a fee of 50% of the original price.
    • Credit is strictly required for commercial use.

Allowed Uses (You MAY):

  • Print the work for personal use (Sticker/Poster).
  • Crop/resize for avatars, headers, or thumbnails.

Prohibited Uses (You may NOT):

  • Remove my watermark or signature.

  • Reproduce the work commercially without purchasing rights.

VIII. CANCELLATION & REFUNDS

  • You may cancel at any time.
    • If cancelled during the sketching stage, a full refund of the down-payment is given
    • If cancelled after the sketch is approved, the refund will be based on the work not done.
  • Once the final artwork is approved and delivered, no refunds are permitted.
  • I reserve the right to cancel the commission at any time. If I cancel, you will receive a full refund.
  • You are prohibited from filing a PayPal chargeback after you have given final approval of the artwork.

IX. WHAT YOU GET

After completion and final approval, you will receive the following as 300 DPI PNG files (unless another format is requested):

  • Final Sketch
  • Final Artwork

Files will be organized in a Google Drive folder and shared via link.

For an additional $2 USD, you may purchase the source file (.PSD or .CLIP), and you will have the rights to give minor edits to the artwork.
